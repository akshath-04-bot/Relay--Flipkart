# Relay: three-minute demo

**To be guided step by step, open `tour.html`:** it highlights every click below. Choose Auto-play to have it click for you. The table below is the same walkthrough for presenting by hand.

Press **Reset demo** first; the clock returns to Day 1, 14:20. You are Joshi Fleet Services (Hadapsar Yard, Node #490). Kaveri Cold Chain Transport has not replied for 6 hours. Gandhi Pharmacy needs 400 kg of insulin, and the window is 5 hours. The Simulation panel ticks off each step as you go.

## Optional checks

- **Self-reactivation:** in Simulation, choose **Reply as Kaveri by SMS**, then reply **1** and **3**. Kaveri returns as Reduced.
- **Capacity:** after T214 and T215 are accepted, T216 is blocked with "Not enough vehicles".
- **Failed attestation:** answer **No, they are reachable**. Custody is refused, and the anchor can be asked to review.
- **Offline:** tick **No data connection**. A banner appears, and actions are logged as "Sent by SMS".
